The standard approach: a cardboard box behind the front desk and a spreadsheet nobody updates. Universities run billion-dollar research programs and process thousands of students a day. Our mission
Every campus loses things. Most have no real system for getting them back. Laika is built for how universities actually work.
How it works
three steps
01
Staff log found items
in 30 seconds
Photo goes in. AI generates a name, description, and value classification automatically. Staff confirm, add a location, and save. Most items are logged in under half a minute. No training required.
02
Students search and
describe their item
Students visit the portal, describe what they lost in plain language, and browse the live inventory. High-value items stay blurred until the student can describe it from memory. Ownership is confirmed before anything is revealed.
03
Ownership verified
before pickup
Staff review the submitted claim, confirm the description matches, and hand over the item in person with ID verification. A full audit trail runs from the moment an item is logged to the moment it leaves the desk.
What Laika does
Built around
how campuses actually work.

Five systems working together so nothing slips through — for staff, for students, for administrators.

AI photo intake
Photograph a found item and Laika instantly generates a name, description, and category. Staff confirm and save in under 30 seconds. No manual data entry.
Plain-language search
Students describe what they lost in their own words. Laika matches it against the live inventory — no exact-match required. High-value items stay blurred until ownership is confirmed.
Automatic SMS alerts
Students register a lost item once. The moment a matching item is logged by staff, they get a text automatically. No checking back, no polling a website.
PIN-verified pickup
Every handover requires student ID and a secure PIN. A complete audit trail is kept from the moment an item is logged to the moment it leaves the desk.
Portal integration
Laika works standalone or integrates into your existing campus portal. Built to fit what you already have, not replace it. Setup takes minutes, not months.
