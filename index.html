What Laika does
Five tools.
One system.

For staff, for students, for the front desk.

AI photo intake
Photograph a found item and Laika instantly generates a name, description, and category. Staff confirm and save in under 30 seconds. No manual data entry.
Plain-language search
Students describe what they lost in their own words. Laika matches it against the live inventory with no exact match required. High-value items stay blurred until ownership is confirmed.
Automatic SMS alerts
Students register a lost item once. The moment a matching item is logged by staff, they get a text automatically. No checking back, no polling a website.
PIN-verified pickup
Every handover requires student ID and a PIN. Full audit trail from log to pickup.
Portal integration
Laika works standalone or integrates into your existing systems. Built to fit what you already have, not replace it. Setup takes minutes, not months.
